DAYS
Awareness over guilt. Clarity over confusion
This app is designed to help you see your year, one day at a time. Instead of numbers and calenders, we use 365 simple dots, each representing a single day of the year. As days pass, they change color, giving you a visual reminder of how much time has moved forward. And how you chose to spend it.
CORE PILLARS
Year Overview
Watch the tapestry of your life unfold through colors as you categorize each passing moment with the liquid surge interaction
Milestones & Goals
Convert abstract ambitions into concrete deadlines. See exactly how many sunrises remain between you and your targets.
Visual Statistics
Reveal hidden rhythms. Analyze patterns of productivity and rest to move forward with data-driven self-awareness.
This app is not meant to create tension, pressure, or anxiety. it's purpose is awareness and improvement, not guilt. However, when there is no sense of time or direction, procrastination can silently take over. A visible timeline works like a gentle deadline, not to stress you, but to remind you.
Even when people work at the last moment, they work because a deadline exists. This app simply makes time visible, so your days don't slip away unnoticed.
